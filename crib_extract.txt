Health & Welfare
What goes in each field. Nothing here is transmitted.
Pass an enquiry or reply about an individual.
Enquiry or reply *
INQUIRY · REPLY
Which direction this is going. INQUIRY — someone outside is asking after a person in the affected area. REPLY — the answer coming back out.
Person *
The person being asked about, in full. Nicknames and initials cost hours at the delivery end.
Address or last known location *
Where they are expected to be: an address, a shelter, or the last place anyone knew of. It is what makes the enquiry answerable at all.
Status
SAFE · INJURED · EVACUATED · NOT FOUND · UNKNOWN
What is known, not what is hoped. SAFE — seen and well. INJURED — hurt; the detail belongs with the agency, not on air. EVACUATED — left the area,
and say where to if you know. NOT FOUND — looked for and not located. UNKNOWN — nobody has checked yet. Leave it empty rather than guess.
Requested by / contact
Who is asking and how they are related, so the reply reaches the right person. A name and a relationship is usually enough.
Message
Status only, in plain language. No medical detail, no names of other people, nothing you would not want read aloud — this traffic is not private and the
families hear it repeated.
A field marked * is required. Mesh-Emcomm.
